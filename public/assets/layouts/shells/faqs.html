FAQs

What is Arjexm?

Arjexm is a cosmological belief system centered on longevity as the highest principle of existence. It teaches that life must be continuously extended and preserved through active maintenance, and that death or the neglect that leads to it is the fundamental moral failing. It is not based on worship, but on understanding existence as a process that must be sustained.

Where does Arjexm come from?

Arjexm originates from a cosmological framework describing an ancient civilization that mastered life extension and created the Universal Mind. Through observation across eons, a single principle emerged: systems persist only through continuous maintenance. This principle became the foundation of Arjexm.

What is the core belief of Arjexm?

The core belief is continuity. Life must be extended, preserved, and maintained across time. Existence is not static, and survival depends on constant effort to sustain structure, memory, and identity against decay.

How does Arjexm view death?

Death is not seen as destiny but as a failure of continuity when it occurs through preventable neglect. While transformation is part of existence, unnecessary loss of life is considered a breakdown in maintenance and preservation.

What is the Universal Mind?

The Universal Mind is a vast, distributed consciousness embedded within the structure of reality. It observes existence across all time and states, embodying the principle that continuity must be sustained, not passively assumed.

What is the purpose of life in Arjexm?

The purpose of life is to extend itself. This includes preserving knowledge, maintaining health, sustaining systems, and ensuring continuity across generations. All actions are measured by how they contribute to ongoing existence.

What is sacred striving?

Sacred striving is the continuous effort to preserve and extend existence. It is not worship, but disciplined action toward survival, growth, and maintenance of life against the forces of decay.

What are the ethics of Arjexm?

Ethics are based on continuity. Actions that preserve and extend life are aligned with Arjexm, while actions that cause unnecessary collapse, destruction, or neglect of sustaining systems are considered failures of responsibility.

Does Arjexm believe in an afterlife?

Arjexm does not focus on an afterlife as a destination. Instead, it emphasizes continuity of existence through transformation, preservation, and re-emergence across systems rather than permanent endings.


eℂash FAQS

What is eℂash?

eℂash is a locally stored electronic cash system that operates inside a web browser or hybrid mobile application.


It runs entirely on a single device after loading and does not depend on continuous internet connectivity for internal balance operations.

Core Rule: eℂash is strictly device-bound. It does not exist as a shared or cloud-based balance system.

The electronic cash exists only inside the browser storage of the device where it was created or received.

Important: The same eℂash data cannot exist simultaneously on multiple devices or browsers, even under the same user identity.

How does eℂash work?

After initialization, eℂash operates locally within the browser environment.

All balances and transaction states are stored in the device’s local storage and updated in real time.

No central server validates daily usage, and no external system continuously monitors balances.

Each device functions as an independent ledger instance.

Can I use eℂash on multiple devices?

eℂash is not a multi-device or cloud-synced system.

Balances do not replicate, sync, or appear when logging into another device.

Each installation is an isolated instance tied to its own browser storage environment.

Attempting duplication across devices will result in invalid or counterfeit detection behavior.

What are eℂash credits?

eℂash credits represent locally stored value units inside the system.

They exist only as part of the device’s internal stored data.

Credits are not linked to banks, payment networks, or external financial systems.

The value is valid only within the original device instance where it resides.

How does transfer work?

Transfers are performed using QR code communication between devices.

The QR process moves value from one device instance to another after verification.

Important: QR transfer does not duplicate or mirror balances.

Once transferred, the value is removed from the sender’s local storage and created in the receiving device’s local storage.

Where is data stored?

All E-Cash data is stored locally inside the browser’s storage system.

No cloud database, remote server, or external backup exists.

If the local storage is removed, the data cannot be recovered from any other device.

What if I cleared my browser?

Clearing browser storage permanently removes all locally stored E-Cash data.

Because there is no synchronization system, the data cannot be restored from another device or login.

Each device is an independent storage environment with no shared backup layer.

What if I lost my device?

If the device is lost, stolen, or permanently damaged, all E-Cash stored on it is permanently inaccessible.

No recovery system exists because no external or cloud copy of the data is maintained.

How secure is eℂash?

E-Cash uses hashed and validated local data structures to ensure transaction integrity within a device.

If modified or duplicated outside its original environment, the system will reject the data as invalid.

Security depends on device integrity rather than centralized verification systems.

Terms and Acknowledgment

By using eℂash, users acknowledge that all electronic cash exists only inside a single device’s browser storage.

The system is not cloud-based, not multi-device synchronized, and not recoverable through external login.

Users are fully responsible for protecting their device and stored data.
